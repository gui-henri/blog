This note will be about how we end up internalizing behaviors without even noticing, including at software engineering. Here i talk about how reading the Agile Manifesto shaped my way of thinking, and this behavior payed off when it was displayed in front of the clients.

Need need to take care of ourselves, read, study, and truly internalize things. As Matt Pocock said:

> If you want to get good at using AI, GET GOOD AT THE THING YOU'RE USING IT FOR.

Because in our field, theory is much underapreciated, despite years and years of books and good articles being published.

Experience is not wisdom, having the years is not worth it if you still behave like an intern. A good theory backing the practice will give you precision. You will be able to clearly say why were having X over Y, not just because *"i did like this in the past and it did or didn't worked"*. You can think a good reason for yourself, but probably an theory backing it up already exists.

What is the theory guiding you actions? You have to respond to this clearly, so you can justify your actions.
