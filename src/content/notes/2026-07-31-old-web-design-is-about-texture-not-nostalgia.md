Designing this blog I tried to reach the old web without being retro for retro's sake.

The trick: the old web's charm was *texture* — paper-like backgrounds, subtle borders, panel
frames, serif type — not its layouts. Modern mobile-first layouts with those textures read as
"timeless" instead of "dated".

Warm cream instead of white, thin rules instead of heavy shadows, a grain instead of flat
fill. That's the whole palette.
