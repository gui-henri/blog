# Repurpose Matrix

One essay becomes many artifacts. The essay is the source of truth; everything else is a
rendering of it for a specific audience and surface.

## From one essay

| Artifact | Derived from | Surface | Effort | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thread (6–10 posts) | The essay's outline | X | 20–30 min | Before publishing the essay — "teaser" |
| "Working on this" post | Progress, screenshots | X / LinkedIn / Instagram | 10 min | During the writing process |
| Professional post | The problem & the lesson, no jargon | LinkedIn | 15 min | After publishing |
| Carousel (5–8 slides) | Key points, one slide per point | Instagram | 30–40 min | After publishing |
| Short video (30–60 s) | One idea, one example | YouTube / Instagram / TikTok | 30 min | After publishing |
| Long video | The essay, spoken | YouTube | 1–2 h (recording + edit) | When the topic is deep enough |

## The "during the process" rule

Publishing is not reserved for the finished artifact. Capture the journey:

1. **Starting** — "I'm studying X." (capture)
2. **Middle** — "Hit a weird bug in X. Here's the failure." (note)
3. **Breakthrough** — "X finally clicked. The trick was Y." (note)
4. **Shipped** — the essay / project (post)

Each of these is independently useful and costs minutes. Four small artifacts instead of
waiting weeks for one perfect one.

## Checklist before an essay ships

- [ ] Essay drafted and published here.
- [ ] X thread written (teaser).
- [ ] LinkedIn post written.
- [ ] Carousel or short video planned (or explicitly skipped).
- [ ] Video script written (or explicitly skipped).
- [ ] Repurpose backlog updated in [tracking/](./tracking/).
