# Video script

The essay, spoken. One argument, one example, one payoff.

## Hook (0:00–0:20)

The single most surprising thing, stated plainly. No cold start.

## Setup (0:20–1:30)

The problem in words a smart non-expert understands. Why it's worth caring about.

## The example (1:30–5:00)

One concrete example carried through the whole video. Show the failure first, then the fix.

## The payoff (5:00–6:00)

The rule or principle extracted from the example. Repeatable, quotable.

## CTA (6:00–end)

One next step. Point to the essay for the full details.
