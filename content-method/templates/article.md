## The problem

What was broken, missing, or confusing? Start from the reader's frustration.

## Context

Who this is for, and the landscape around the topic. Set the scene without padding.

## The approach

How I tackled it. The reasoning, the dead ends, the decisions. This is where the thinking lives.

## The details

The actual work: code, steps, configuration, numbers. Concrete enough to reproduce.

## Results

What happened. What it measured like, what it felt like. Honest numbers beat impressive ones.

## Lessons

The 2–3 things worth stealing from this essay. If the reader remembers only this section, make it worth it.
